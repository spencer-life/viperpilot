Product roadmap · ViperPilot · 2026-09-29

Fast profile switching for the Viper V4 Pro.

The immediate priority is a lightweight native tray and hotkey path. User-authored profile drafts are also part of the product. Device writes stay limited to documented Viper V4 Pro fields and supervised evidence gates.

Current checkpoint

Later documentation revisions have separate CI/security status. These are software-only checks; production UI and owner-present mouse gates remain open.

Evidence boundary: Saved aliases, profile drafts, plans, GET results, and synthetic previews are not onboard slots or proof of a hardware write. Full Synapse parity and support for all recent Razer mice are not established requirements.

Definition of a release

Evidence closes the gate.

Keep software checks, desktop usability, and measured device behavior distinct. Plans and visual previews do not prove device support.

  1. Software. Checks pass for the exact candidate and are reported as hardware-free where applicable.
  2. Desktop. Owner-present tray, editor, accessibility, presentation, and performance gates are recorded.
  3. Hardware. Full identity, immutable baseline, one-field plan, independent readback, and restoration are recorded.
  4. Regression. Both complete built-in profiles and production hotkey pass; process-exit and power-cycle behavior are recorded before persistence claims.
  5. Scope. Public claims stay within the documented Viper V4 Pro field scope and tested conditions.

ViperPilot is the project name. Existing executable and Cargo package identifiers may remain for compatibility. Current software and hardware validation boundaries are in quick-switch status and the supervised checklist.
